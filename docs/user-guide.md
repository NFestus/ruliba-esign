# User guide

Ruliba Clays eSign replaces printing, signing by hand, scanning and sending by courier. You upload a document once; everyone signs on their computer or phone; everyone gets the signed copy by email. This guide is for Ruliba staff. The last section is a one-page note you can send to suppliers.

Open eSign at **https://esign.ruliba.com** and sign in with your work email and password.

## 1. Your dashboard

- **Waiting for you**: documents you need to sign or approve. Start here every morning.
- **In progress**: documents you sent that are still being signed or approved.
- **Completed** and **Drafts**.
- The **bell** at the top right tells you when someone signs, approves, returns or declines. Click a message to open the document.

## 2. Send a document for signing

You need the Sender role. It takes about two minutes.

**1. Upload.** **New document**, drop a PDF or Word file (up to 25 MB), give it a clear title and choose the document type. If eSign says the type may not be signed electronically (for example a power of attorney, or anything about land and buildings), use the paper process for that one.

**2. Signers.** Choose **Internal** or **External** for each person:

- **Internal (Ruliba staff):** search the list of colleagues who can sign and click **Add**. Not in the list? Click **Add a new colleague** and enter their name and work email: they get an eSign account and an email to activate it, and they can sign once it is active (their invitation includes the activation link). Staff open the emailed link, sign in to eSign (with two-step sign-in if they use it) and land on the document; no emailed code. They can also sign from their dashboard.
- **External (outside Ruliba):** type the person's full name and email. Past contacts are suggested as you type. They sign from an emailed link with a one-time code and need no account.

Then choose the order:

- **One after another**: the second person is emailed only after the first has signed.
- **All at once**: everyone is emailed together.
- **In groups**: give people the same step number to sign at the same time.

Change "Signs" to **Gets a copy** for people who only need the signed file (for example the archive mailbox).

**3. Fields.** Pick a signer on the left, choose a field (Signature, Initials, Stamp, Date signed, Name, Text, Checkbox, Dropdown) and click on the page where it goes. Drag to move it, drag the corner to resize, and click the red bin on a field (or press Delete) to remove it. Each signer needs at least one Signature or Initials field. "Date signed" and "Name" fill in by themselves.

Use **Stamp** where a company stamp is needed. A Ruliba signer chooses one of the official stamps the administrator allows them; a supplier uploads a photo or scan of their own company stamp.

**4. Send.** Check the list, fill in the supplier and the **contract value** if there is one, write a short message, choose when the link stops working and how often to remind people. Then:

- **Send for signing**: the reference number is given and the first signers are emailed.
- **Send for approval** (supply contracts and other types that need approval): the box on the right shows who approves, in order. The approvers are asked first; the signers are emailed and the reference number given only when the last one approves.

Use **Save draft** at any time and come back later from Drafts.

**Tip:** if you send the same kind of document often, click **Save as a template for next time** on the Send step (see section 6).

## 3. Follow a document

Open it from the dashboard. You see:

- **Approval** (if needed) and **People**, with each person's status: Invited, Opened, Signed.
- **Details**: supplier, value, dates, reminders.
- **Files**: the original, and when finished the **signed and sealed copy** and the **certificate of completion**.
- **Activity**: every step with time and IP address. This record cannot be changed by anyone.

From here you can:

- **Send link again** to someone who lost the email. Their old link stops working.
- **Cancel document** (with a reason) if something is wrong. Everyone is told and the links stop working. Then correct it and send a new one.

If a document is **returned for changes** by an approver, you get an email with the reason and it is back in your Drafts. Correct it and send it again; approval starts over.

If a signer **declines**, you get their reason by email. Correct the document and send a new one.

## 4. Sign a document (staff)

When something is waiting for you, click **Sign** on the dashboard (or the button in the email).

1. Read the document. Your fields are highlighted.
2. Tick that you agree to sign electronically.
3. Click each field. For your signature choose **Draw**, **Type** (your name in handwriting style) or **Upload** a picture of your signature. You can save it under Settings → My signature to reuse it.
4. Click **Finish signing**.

Because you are signed in to eSign, you do not need an emailed code. If you cannot sign it as it is, click **Decline** and say why.

**Stamps.** If the document has a Stamp box for you, tap it and choose the Ruliba stamp you are allowed to use. If none is listed, ask the administrator: only people named in Settings → Stamps may apply a stamp.

## 5. Approve a document

You need the Approver role. You are emailed, and the document shows under Waiting for you with **Review**.

1. Open it and read it (use the file links under Files).
2. **Approve** (you can add a comment, for example "Prices match the tender") or **Return for changes** with what needs to change. Your reason is emailed to the sender.

Approving is not signing: your approval is recorded in the activity log and on the signing record.

## 6. Templates

**Templates** in the menu shows the templates you can use: your department's and the shared ones.

- **Use template**: type the document title and who fills each role (e.g. Supplier: name and email). eSign creates a draft with the file and fields already placed. Check it and send.
- **To make one**: prepare a document as usual, and on the Send step click **Save as a template for next time**. Name each signer by role ("Supplier", "Finance Director") and choose whether all departments can use it.

## 6a. Folders

On **Documents**, use **+ New folder** to make your own folders (e.g. "Supply contracts 2026", "Kigali Builders"). To file a document, open it and choose the folder under **Details → My folder**. Click a folder at the top of Documents to see only what is in it, or **Not in a folder** for the rest.

- Folders are yours alone: colleagues do not see them, and a folder never changes who can see a document.
- A document can be in one of your folders at a time; choosing another folder moves it.
- **Rename** or **Delete folder** appear when a folder is selected. Deleting a folder does not delete its documents.

## 7. Check a document

**Check a document** in the menu (or https://esign.ruliba.com/check, which anyone can use without signing in): upload a PDF to learn whether it is exactly a copy signed through eSign, unchanged. Send suppliers and banks this link if they want to confirm a contract.

## 8. Your account

**Settings → Your account**: change your password, and turn on **two-step sign-in** (recommended for everyone who sends contracts) with an authenticator app or text messages to your phone. **Forgot your password?** Use the link on the sign-in page; we email you a link to choose a new one. A new password must be different from your last 5. **Settings → My signature**: save your signature and initials.

Never share your password. Ruliba IT will never ask for it.

## 9. Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who does the email come from? | From sign@ruliba.com, showing your name ("Your Name via Ruliba eSign"). If the supplier presses Reply, the answer comes to your own mailbox |
| The supplier did not get the email | Ask them to look in spam; then use **Send link again** |
| The supplier's code does not work | Codes last 10 minutes; they should use the newest email. They can ask for a new code once a minute |
| Can I change a document after sending? | No. Cancel it and send a corrected one. This protects everyone: what was signed is exactly what was sent |
| Is an e-signature legal? | Yes, for most business documents under Rwandan law (Law 18/2010). Not for wills, powers of attorney, cheques and similar, documents about land and buildings, or notarised deeds: eSign refuses those types |
| Where is the signed copy kept? | In eSign, on Ruliba's servers in Rwanda, for the period set for that document type. Everyone also gets it by email |

## Note for suppliers and partners (to send with your first document)

> **Signing a Ruliba Clays document electronically**
>
> You will receive an email from **sign@ruliba.com** titled "Please sign: …". You do not need an account or any software; a phone or computer with internet is enough.
>
> 1. Click **Review and sign** in the email.
> 2. Ask for a one-time code. It is emailed to you at once and works for 10 minutes. Type it in.
> 3. Read the document and tick that you agree to sign electronically.
> 4. Click each highlighted field and add your signature (draw it with your finger or mouse, type your name, or upload a picture). If there is a **Stamp** box, upload a clear photo or scan of your company stamp.
> 5. Click **Finish signing**.
>
> When everyone has signed, you receive the signed PDF by email, with a signing record page. It is digitally sealed by Ruliba Clays Ltd; to confirm it is genuine, upload it at **https://esign.ruliba.com/check**.
>
> If something in the document is wrong, click **Decline** and say why: the sender is told at once.
>
> Questions: reply to the email or contact sign@ruliba.com.
